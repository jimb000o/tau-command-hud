Why this is not a normal blockchain

On other chains, code is law. On Tau, the agreement is law: users write the rules in logic, every rule is checked before it can apply, and nothing changes without the validators’ consent. There is no admin key.

Experimental · no claim of accuracy

      
Tau HUD is an experimental community approach to visualising the Tau testnet. We do not claim that the information shown is accurate, complete or current, in simulation or live mode. It is not official information, not financial advice and not technical advice. Check anything that matters against the tau-testnet repository and your own node.

      

      

      
Where each claim comes from

      
Each principle card at the top carries its source. seen on the testnet means we read it from the community node; from the protocol means it is documented in tau-testnet (README and code, pinned to commit 539d1e2) but we have not watched it happen on the real chain yet. The link on each card goes to the line that backs it. Refusals matter as much as the happy path: the simulation deliberately produces proposals that do not compile, rule offers that collide, and proposals that miss quorum and expire (README §Governance ↗); the “n refused” chip on Transactions filters to what Tau refused.

      

      

      
Simulation vs. live node

      
The simulation follows the documented protocol of tau-testnet: validators vote on rule changes, accounts write and share their own Tau rules, every block is judged by Tau. It is a model, not the real chain.

      
Live mode speaks the node's raw WebSocket protocol (getblocks, getmempool, getgovernance). The default address wss://node.tauhud.com is a read-only community relay in front of a Tau testnet node run for this page (its setup is public). To read your own node instead, run it with this page's origin in TAU_WS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS and put its address in the field.
