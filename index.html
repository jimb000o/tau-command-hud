Why this is not a normal blockchain

On other chains, code is law. On Tau, the agreement is law: users write the rules in logic, every rule is checked before it can apply, and nothing changes without the validators’ consent. There is no admin key.

Checked before it appliesA proposed rule is compiled on its own first. If it does not hold, it never enters the chain.see it: Rule changes · “compiled in isolation ✓”
No admin keyNobody can change the rules alone. A change needs the validators’ agreement and takes effect at a later block.see it: Validators · “agreement needed”
Enforced against the key holderYour rule binds your account, not your device. A stolen key still obeys your limits.see it: Transactions · red “✕ refused”
Money with termsA transfer can be returnable until a release point, locked until a time, or co-signed above an amount.see it: Personal money rules

About this page

      
Unofficial community projectBuilt by Tau community members. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or maintained by IDNI or the Tau team. Data in simulation mode is generated in your browser and is not the real chain.

      
Experimental · no claim of accuracy

      
Tau HUD is an experimental community approach to visualising the Tau testnet. We do not claim that the information shown is accurate, complete or current, in simulation or live mode. It is not official information, not financial advice and not technical advice. Check anything that matters against the tau-testnet repository and your own node.

      
Simulation vs. live node

      
The simulation follows the documented protocol of tau-testnet: validators vote on rule changes, accounts write and share their own Tau rules, every block is judged by Tau. It is a model, not the real chain.

      
Live mode speaks the node's raw WebSocket protocol (getblocks, getmempool, getgovernance). The public testnet bootnode only exposes P2P, so run your own read node (Docker, see the README), allow this page's origin with TAU_WS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS, then switch to Live node.
