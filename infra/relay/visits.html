Hour of day (UTC)

Counted without cookies and without storing anything in the visitor's browser. A visitor is recognised for one day only, by a hash of the address and browser with a random salt that is kept in memory and replaced at 00:00 UTC; addresses are never stored. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and bots are not counted. Page views from the same person on two days count as two visitors. To leave yourself out, open https://tauhud.com/?nostats=1 once on each of your browsers (?nostats=0 undoes it).
