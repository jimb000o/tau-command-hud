Five requirements. Three of them cannot all hold.

A brief can contradict itself, and whoever builds from it, a person or an AI, finds out late. Tau Language is made for requirements: write each one as a line of logic and a Tau engine says whether they can all hold together, and if not, which ones clash. Press the button, then switch requirements off and ask again.

    What is checked, and what is not

    1. 1
      The check is exact. Tau does not guess. For the lines it gets, the answer is a proof: they can hold together, or they cannot.
    2. 2
      The translation is not. The Tau line under each requirement was written by hand for this page. Whoever writes that line, a person or a language model, can get it wrong, and Tau only sees the line.
    3. 3
      Small on purpose. Tau Language is an alpha. This box answers within 5 seconds and takes 2 KB at most.
