Guardian

A second key for your test wallet: a code from your phone before a payment goes out.

Your account's own rule says "the Guardian co-signs my payments". Small ones up to a daily allowance it signs by itself; for the rest you type the 6-digit code from your Authenticator app here, and it co-signs on Tau Testnet Alpha. It can say yes to your payment, never send one, and it holds no key of yours. Test AGRS have no value.

Simulation is on. The Guardian works only on the real testnet: switch the HUD to Live to connect your app.

1 Connect your Authenticator app

Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password or any app that shows 6-digit codes. Your wallet signs once to show it is your account; then scan the QR code and type the first code.

The key stays on our server and never goes on the chain. Anyone who sees it can make your codes, so keep it to yourself.

2 What the Guardian does

Your account's rule sends payments to the Guardian. It signs by itself what you allow here and waits for your code for the rest. Amounts in test AGRS.

    3 Confirm a payment

      A payment from the wallet that the Guardian does not sign by itself waits here for your code. A payment nobody confirms runs out after about 1,000 blocks and the amount stays with you (the fee is spent).

      What it can do, and what it cannot
      • ✓It co-signs only payments your own account asked for, and only after the right code. It cannot send, move or hold anything.
      • ✓The code never goes on the chain: it comes to our server, which checks it and signs. Each code works once; five wrong codes lock your account for 15 minutes.
      • !With "only payments above", many smaller payments are not stopped: a testnet rule sees only the payment at hand. The other two settings send every payment past the Guardian.
      • !Whoever holds your wallet key can still change or remove the rule. The Guardian guards against a mistaken or tricked payment, not against a stolen key.
      • !If our server is down, payments that wait for your code run out unconfirmed; they never go through without the code.
      • !Tau Testnet Alpha holds personal rules for 2 accounts at a time, so the Guardian guards at most two accounts at once for now.
