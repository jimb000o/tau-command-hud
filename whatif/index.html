    How the sandbox works: validators take turns in a fixed order; a block reaches the others after the network delay; a maker builds on the highest block it has seen, so a late block leads to two blocks at one height and the longer branch wins. Rule-change votes are transactions that wait for a block; a change passes with enough yes votes before its deadline of 20 blocks, as on the testnet (governance.py ↗).
