Try Tau in four steps.

On Tau the rules change by agreement, and every account can carry a rule of its own that the chain itself checks. Try it on Tau Testnet Alpha: a few minutes in this browser, nothing to install. Test AGRS have no value.

Where this happens

Every step is a real transaction on Tau Testnet Alpha, signed in your browser. This page only reads what your wallet did and what the chain says.

  • Your key stays here. It lives in this browser; back it up in the wallet if you want to keep the account.
  • Nothing is sold. Test AGRS have no value and are only for trying things out.
  • Two rule places. The testnet holds personal rules for 2 accounts at a time; the line is fair, and the simulation is always open.
  • Unofficial. Built by Tau community members, not by IDNI or the Tau team.
