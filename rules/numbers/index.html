What no personal rule does

  • No guard for a stolen key. Whoever holds the key can sign a new rule. Rules guard against mistaken or tricked payments.
  • No total per day. A rule sees one payment at a time, never the ones before it.
  • Small on purpose. At most 32 comparisons and 8 KiB per rule.
