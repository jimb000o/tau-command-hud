How agreeing works

  • Take it. “Take this rule” fills your wallet with the same rule. You sign it, and from the next block every node checks your payments against it.
  • Counted from the chain. Each account that runs the same rule adds a dot. Nobody can add one without signing.
  • Two places at a time. When both are taken the wallet puts you in a fair line, or you take the rule on the simulation.
  • Change it first. The wallet shows the rule in its builder; adjust the limit or the names before you sign.
