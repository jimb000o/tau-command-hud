    Known issues testnet bugs that change what you see · all our findings

    Some “waiting” transactions are already in a block

    The testnet node keeps some transactions in its mempool as pending for up to 60 minutes although they are already sealed in a block. The waiting dots in the HUD’s chain core come from that mempool, so a few of them are not really waiting. Not a HUD bug; reported as IDNI/tau-testnet#81.

    The testnet restarts without notice

    IDNI sometimes restarts the testnet on the same genesis. Our node notices, wipes the old chain and syncs the new one; the HUD starts again at block 1. Restarts are marked above. Asked for an announcement in IDNI/tau-testnet#82.
