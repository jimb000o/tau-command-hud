Run a node

A server with 2 CPU, 4 GB RAM, 40 GB disk (Ubuntu or Debian, x86 or ARM, about €6 a month). As root:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jimb000o/tau-command-hud/main/kit/install.sh | NODE_NAME=my-node bash

Pick your own name. The first build takes about 1.5 hours; then your node appears here within a minute. Full guide
