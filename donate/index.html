What a donation is not

  • Not a purchase: it buys no access, no feature and no say.
  • Not a promise: nothing is owed in return, including that the site keeps running.
  • Not an investment: no share, no reward, no return.

Each donation is public on Ethereum. The shortened sender address and the amount are shown here and thanked in the Telegram channel. Privacy notice, section 11 ›
