Donations

Tau HUD is free. Donations are voluntary, and nothing is owed in return.

An independent community project, built by one person in their free time and not affiliated with IDNI. If you find it useful, a donation is welcome. Below: what it costs, every donation received with its transaction on Ethereum, and the address.

What a donation is not

  • Not a purchase: it buys no access, no feature and no say.
  • Not a promise: nothing is owed in return, including that the site keeps running.
  • Not an investment: no share, no reward, no return.
