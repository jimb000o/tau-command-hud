Crew

The Tau HUD crew is a paid membership. It is a purchase, not a donation.

Tau HUD stays free for everyone. This page keeps two things apart: a membership you pay for every month, and voluntary support with nothing in return.

Paid membership

  • Billed every month. The membership renews each month and runs until you cancel it.
  • Sold by Fourthwall. The seller and your contract partner is Fourthwall, Inc., which runs our external shop. Tau HUD does not take the payment.
  • What you get. A discount on everything in the shop, on two levels. The shop has no products yet, so today a membership is mostly a way to back this site.
  • Who earns from it. The person who runs Tau HUD receives income from the memberships.
  • Before you buy. Prices, renewal, cancellation and your right of withdrawal are shown with the offer in the shop.

Voluntary support, nothing in return

A donation buys nothing and is not a membership. It needs a crypto wallet and goes straight to the address shown on the donations page.
