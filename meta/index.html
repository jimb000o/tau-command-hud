Most visited pages

Counted on our own server, not by another company: a page sends one small request when it opens, one after 30 seconds in view and one when you leave it with how long it was in view (only a rough step such as “1–3 min” is kept). Nothing is stored in your browser, addresses are never stored, and browsers with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted. A visitor is recognised for one day only. Data: JSON.
