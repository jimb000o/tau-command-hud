What you love most on Tau HUD.

Every ♥ on the HUD is a real transaction on the Tau Net testnet, from the test wallet in a visitor’s browser to one box with no key behind it. We read the box to learn which parts people value most, and build those first. It counts wallets, not people: whoever makes several wallets can give several hearts. Test AGRS have no value.

Hearts per part

Reading the hearts on the testnet…

    Read from the chain by our relay at node.tauhud.com, every two minutes. A new heart shows once its block is sealed.

    A heart counts for the level its wallet chose on Tau HUD when it was given; hearts from before 14 Nov 2026 carry no level and count only under Everyone.

    The hearts stay when the testnet starts a new chain: our relay keeps them.

    How a heart works

    Click ♥ in a panel of the HUD. The first time, a short note says what happens; your browser makes a test wallet if it has none. Ten seconds after your last click it sends one transaction for each part whose heart changed:

    100 + parta heart for that part (101 is part 1, the chain core)
    200 + partthe heart taken back
    + 300 × levelthe level chosen on Tau HUD: 1 Beginner, 2 Intermediate, 3 Expert (401 is a beginner’s heart for part 1)

    For each wallet and part only the newest of these counts, so clicking a hundred times gives at most one heart. A transaction carries no memo on this testnet, so the amount is the message.
