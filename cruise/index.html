What the kinds mean: pays a plain transfer to the place’s till; books a transfer the passenger can take back until a set time (retractable_transfer); cancels takes such a booking back (retract_transfer); bills one transaction that pays several places at once; books ahead a retractable transfer of the fare to the ship’s till days before a call, released at the arrival; refunds takes it back before the arrival; escrow how each booking ended on the chain: paid out to the till or refunded to the passenger; tries a trick a pirate sends something the chain must refuse, and stopped shows that it did, with its answer.
