How hard the testnet is working.

Every move in White Rabbit Key is a real test transfer, so the more people play, the more the testnet has to carry. This page shows what it carries, minute by minute, for the last six hours: what gets sealed, what waits, and how long a transaction waits for its block.

Records since the relay began counting

One validator, run by IDNI, seals every block; it builds one when ten transactions wait or thirty seconds have passed. Our relay lets visitors send at most 60 transactions a minute in all and the island bot at most 24, so a busy minute here says as much about our limits as about the testnet’s. A transaction sealed but still listed (a ghost, IDNI issue #81) counts as waiting until it leaves the list.
